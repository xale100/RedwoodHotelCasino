## 2. Color Palette — LOCKED

### Design Rationale

The proposed palette keyword — old-growth canopy, Klamath River, coastal fog, salmon, condors — maps to a very specific place. Cross-referencing the 11 reference sites, the strongest visual precedent is the **Post Ranch Inn + Visit Yurok Country axis**: dark forest green primary, warm cream secondary, amber/natural accent, with coastal-fog cool tones.

This combination is:
- Rare in the casino-hotel space (most use generic dark-luxury charcoal or bright Vegas palletes)
- Directly evocative of the specific land (not a fantasy version of it)
- Capable of supporting both the casino's warmth and the sacred/cultural content simultaneously
- Validated by Snoqualmie Casino's successful use of deep forest-green on a tribal property

### Locked Palette

| Token | Name | Hex | Rationale |
|-------|------|-----|-----------|
| `--canopy` | Canopy | `#0F1A0F` | Primary dark background — old-growth canopy at dusk; differentiating in the casino-hotel space |
| `--bark` | Bark | `#2C1508` | Redwood bark — warm dark brown for depth and warmth beside the green |
| `--river` | River | `#4E7A5C` | Klamath River sage-green — mid-tone, used for borders, hover states, accents |
| `--salmon` | Salmon | `#C86040` | Salmon run orange — warm primary accent; casino energy + ecological identity |
| `--fog` | Fog | `#8CA8B0` | Coastal fog blue-gray — cool secondary accent; coastal geography, horizon light |
| `--parchment` | Parchment | `#EDE5D8` | Warm cream — type on dark, light section backgrounds, card surfaces |
| `--mist` | Mist | `#FAF7F2` | Near-white — bright section backgrounds; warmer than #FFF |
| `--elk-gold` | Elk Gold | `#C89440` | Premium warm gold — CTAs on dark backgrounds, hover states, star ratings |
| `--white` | White | `#FFFFFF` | Pure white for high-contrast type on very dark backgrounds |
| `--text-dark` | Text | `#1A1A1A` | Near-black for body text on light backgrounds |

### Usage Notes
- **Dark sections (hero, casino, culture):** Canopy `#0F1A0F` background, Parchment or White type, Elk Gold or Salmon accent
- **Light sections (rooms, dining, reviews):** Mist `#FAF7F2` or Parchment `#EDE5D8` background, Text Dark `#1A1A1A` body, River `#4E7A5C` accent
- **Casino energy sections:** Bark `#2C1508` background, Salmon `#C86040` headline, Elk Gold `#C89440` CTA
- **Cultural/tribe sections:** Canopy `#0F1A0F` background, Parchment type, River `#4E7A5C` accent, photography-forward

## 3. Typography — LOCKED

### Display: Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts — free)

**Why it works for RHC:**
- Has a pen-calligraphy origin — handcrafted, not mechanically geometric
- High thick-thin contrast reads as cinematic at large sizes on dark backgrounds (confirmed by Post Ranch Inn's Didone-family heading behavior)
- Warm and grounded rather than cold fashion luxury (Bodoni/Didot read colder)
- Multiple weights (Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold) + italic variants give editorial range
- Not overused in the tribal casino / NorCal hotel space — ownable
- Works in both the "casino energy" register and the "sacred land" register without feeling wrong in either

**Alternatives considered and ruled out:**
- Playfair Display: Too widely used in Squarespace boutique hospitality — not distinctive enough
- DM Serif Display: More geometric, less cinematic warmth
- Canela / Freight Display: Premium paid fonts (good alternatives if budget allows later)
- Recoleta: Too playful and rounded — conflicts with the sacred/serious content

### UI / Body: Inter (Google Fonts — free)

**Why Inter over alternatives:**
- Maximum legibility across casino UI, forms, game info, hours, pricing — functional content requires a workhorse
- Excellent at 12–14px for fine print (responsible gaming, legal notices)
- Strong numeric rendering for poker tables, slot info, jackpot counters
- DM Sans and Outfit were considered — warmer and more distinctive, but Inter's technical neutrality is the right choice when the display type is as characterful as Cormorant Garamond

### Scale (modular, 1.333 — Perfect Fourth)

| Role | Size | Weight | Face |
|------|------|--------|------|
| Hero headline | 72–96px / 4.5–6rem | 300 Light or 400 Regular | Cormorant Garamond |
| Section headline | 48–56px / 3–3.5rem | 400–500 Medium | Cormorant Garamond |
| Card headline | 28–32px / 1.75–2rem | 500 Medium | Cormorant Garamond |
| Eyebrow / label | 11–12px / 0.75rem | 600 SemiBold, tracked +0.15em | Inter, ALL CAPS |
| Body / paragraph | 16–18px / 1–1.125rem | 400 Regular | Inter |
| UI / nav / button | 14–15px / 0.875–0.9375rem | 500 Medium | Inter |
| Caption / fine print | 12–13px / 0.75–0.8125rem | 400 Regular | Inter |

**Italic use:** Cormorant Garamond Italic for pull quotes, cultural narrative text, land acknowledgment, restaurant descriptions. Not for UI.

## 4. Layout Rhythm — LOCKED

### Content Width
- Max content width: **1280px** (matches the segment; 1440px is too wide for text-heavy cultural content)
- Hero sections: full-bleed (`100vw`) with no max-width constraint on the image
- Text columns: max `720px` for long-form body copy (responsible gaming, tribe page, blog)

### Vertical Spacing Scale

| Context | Desktop | Mobile |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Hero section | 160px top padding | 96px |
| Major section divider | 128px vertical | 80px |
| Standard section padding | 96px vertical | 64px |
| Card / component internal | 40–48px | 24–32px |
| Between elements within a section | 24px | 16px |

### Grid System
- **Desktop:** 12-column, 24px gutters, within 1280px max
- **Tablet (768px):** 8-column, 20px gutters
- **Mobile (375–430px):** 4-column, 16px gutters, 20px outer margins

### Corner Radius
- **Cards / large containers:** `4px` — refined, not toyish; not zero (too harsh for the warm tone)
- **Buttons:** `4px` — matching cards; consistent geometric vocabulary
- **Pill buttons (CTAs only):** `9999px` — reserved for a single primary booking CTA
- **Images / hero:** `0px` — full-bleed images never get radius

### Elevation / Shadow
- Cards: `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.12), 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` — soft, diffuse; no hard shadows
- Modals / dropdowns: `0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.24)`
- No colored shadows (avoid neon glow effects)

### Motion
- Transitions: `200ms ease` for hover states; `350ms ease` for page transitions
- Hero entrance: fade + subtle upward translate (24px → 0px, 600ms, 80ms delay per element)
- No parallax on mobile — performance constraint
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`

## 5. Visual Tone & Photography Direction

### What the photography must feel like
Based on the Visit Yurok Country and Post Ranch Inn photography benchmarks:
- **Light quality:** Soft natural light — early morning mist, golden hour, overcast coastal light. Not harsh midday sun.
- **Color temperature:** Warm, slightly muted, earthy. Not over-saturated. Film-adjacent.
- **Subjects:** Real people — tribal members as hosts and guides, guests in the landscape, not posed on white backgrounds.
- **Landscape:** Klamath River, old-growth redwoods, coastal bluffs. Wide-format, cinematic aspect ratios (16:9 and 2.35:1 cinematic crops for heroes).
- **Casino:** Warm interior light, motion blur suggesting energy, intimate rather than barn-scale.

### Hero image direction
- Single full-viewport image or looping video (15–30 seconds, no sound)
- Dark gradient overlay: `linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(15,26,15,0.2) 0%, rgba(15,26,15,0.6) 100%)`
- Headline in Parchment, hero CTA in Elk Gold

## 6. Component Vocabulary

### Navigation
- Sticky top nav, transparent over hero, transitions to `--canopy` solid on scroll at 60px
- Height: 72px desktop, 64px mobile
- Logo: left-aligned wordmark (Cormorant Garamond, tracked)
- Links: Inter 500, 14px, Parchment on dark
- "Book Now": pill CTA, Elk Gold fill, Bark text, right of nav
- Mobile: hamburger → full-screen overlay, Canopy background

### Cards (Stay/Play/Eat/Explore)
- 4px radius, Parchment background (light) or Bark/Canopy (dark)
- Category label: Inter 500 ALL CAPS, 11px, River color, +0.15em tracking
- Headline: Cormorant Garamond 500, 28px
- Body: Inter 400, 16px, `--text-dark`
- CTA: text link with arrow, Inter 500, Salmon color on light; Elk Gold on dark

### Buttons
- **Primary (Book Now):** Pill, Elk Gold `#C89440` fill, Bark `#2C1508` text, 14px Inter 500
- **Secondary:** 4px radius, outlined, Parchment or Salmon border + text on dark; River border + text on light
- **Ghost (hero overlays):** Outlined Parchment, `hover` → Parchment fill + Canopy text
- Never more than one primary button in a view

### Section Eyebrows
- ALL CAPS, Inter 600, 11px, letter-spacing 0.15em, River `#4E7A5C` on dark / Bark on light
- Placed 16px above the section headline

## 7. Cultural Design Guidelines

### What NOT to do
- No generic "Native American pattern" borders or backgrounds (feathers, dream catchers, generic geometric patterns attributed to no specific tradition)
- No stock photography of tribal members from other nations
- No tokenizing color symbolism ("red = blood, earth") without specific Yurok cultural validation
- No land acknowledgment in a disclaimer box — it belongs woven into the voice, not isolated

### What TO do
- Photography: Yurok people as the primary subjects in active, dignified roles
- Yurok language (Yurok/Pulikla): use specific words where applicable — `huem-chor` (welcome) in hero, pronunciation guides
- Land as identity: the river, the redwoods, the condors are not backdrop — they are subjects
- Tribal seal: use the official Yurok Tribe seal or a licensed mark in the footer (not in a decorative capacity)
- Tribe page: must be reviewed by a Yurok cultural expert before launch — design a review gate into the build process
